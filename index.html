A neighbourhood cafe that doesn't rush you out the door.

Uncle Chad Cafe & Restro sits on the first floor at D-46, RDC — a clean, well-lit room done up for exactly the kind of evening you're planning, whether that's a family dinner, a first date, or an office get-together that runs long. Every dish on the menu is vegetarian, from the wood-fired pizzas to the tandoor.

Regulars come back for the Baked Pasta, Amritsari Chaap and the Special Pizza — and stay for the shisha, which locals rate as some of the best around Raj Nagar.
